# Disputes and chargebacks SOP

Sample internal knowledge base for the demo. Brightline Studio is a fictional company.

## Response time

Respond to every new dispute within 3 business days, even when the provider's own deadline is longer.
Disputes that reach the provider's review stage without a response from us are almost always lost.

## Evidence checklist

- Signed statement of work or the accepted quote
- Delivery proof: final-file handover email, download logs, or shipping tracking for physical items
- Client communication showing approval of the work (screenshots are fine)
- The invoice and any change requests

## When to accept instead of fighting

Accept the claim and refund when the disputed amount is under $25, or when we can't produce delivery proof.
Anything over $500 needs the finance lead's approval before accepting or making an offer.

## Offers and partial refunds

For "not as described" disputes where some work was delivered, offer a partial refund first (typically
30-50%). Escalate to the provider only after the buyer rejects the offer or doesn't respond within 5 days.

## Tone with buyers

Keep messages short, factual and polite. Never argue in the dispute thread; state what was delivered, attach
evidence, and propose a resolution.
