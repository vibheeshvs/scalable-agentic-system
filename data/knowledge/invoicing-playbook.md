# Invoicing playbook

Sample internal knowledge base for the demo. Brightline Studio is a fictional company.

## Payment terms

Standard terms are Net 15: the invoice is due 15 days after the invoice date. Retainer clients and anything
above $5,000 go out as Net 30. Always put the due date on the invoice rather than only writing "Net 15" in the
note, because clients read the date and ignore the note.

## What every invoice must include

- The client's billing email (the address that pays, not necessarily the person you worked with)
- One line item per deliverable with quantity and unit price, never a single "services" line
- Currency. We bill in USD unless the contract says EUR
- The project code in the memo field, e.g. `BL-2026-014`, so finance can match payments

## Reminders

Send the first reminder 3 days after the due date and a second one 10 days after. After the second reminder
the account manager calls the client; we don't send a third automated reminder.

## Late fees

A late fee of 1.5% of the outstanding amount is added after 30 days overdue, only if the contract includes
the late-fee clause. Check the contract before adding it; adding a fee the contract doesn't allow causes
more disputes than it recovers.

## Cancelling and correcting invoices

A draft invoice can be edited or deleted freely. Once an invoice has been sent, never delete it: cancel it
(the client gets a cancellation notice) and issue a new one. If the client paid outside PayPal (bank
transfer, cash), record the payment on the invoice so it shows as paid instead of cancelling it.
