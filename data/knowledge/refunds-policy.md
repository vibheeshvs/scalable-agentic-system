# Refund policy

Sample internal knowledge base for the demo. Brightline Studio is a fictional company.

## Eligibility

Clients can request a full refund within 14 days of payment if no deliverable has been handed over. After a
deliverable has been handed over, refunds are partial and proportional to the undelivered work.

## Approvals

- Up to $200: any account manager can issue the refund
- $200 to $1,000: needs the project lead's approval
- Above $1,000: needs the finance lead's approval

## How refunds are issued

Refund the original payment (the capture) rather than sending a new payment, so fees are handled correctly
and the refund is linked to the original transaction in reports. Refunds on invoices that were paid outside
PayPal are recorded on the invoice as an external refund.

## Currency

Refunds are always issued in the currency of the original payment.
